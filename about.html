About Mini Game Arcade

Simple, responsive, high-performance web gaming without commercial bloat.

Our Perspective on Web Gaming

Mini Game Arcade was founded on a straightforward belief: web gaming should be instant, responsive, and respectful of the player's time and device.

Too many modern gaming websites have become overburdened with intrusive advertisements, deceptive click traps, slow-loading frameworks, and mandatory account sign-ups. We wanted to build the opposite: an indie arcade platform where games launch in milliseconds, look distinct, and feel great to play.

Core Engineering Principles

  • Zero Installations: Every game runs entirely within modern browsers using native HTML5 Canvas 2D and JavaScript. No app stores, no plugins, no downloads.
  • Calibrated Input: Controls are tested to ensure responsive tactile feedback whether swiping with fingers on a smartphone screen or making razor-sharp mouse movements on desktop.
  • Original Craft: We design original vector visuals and procedural Web Audio synthesizers, avoiding copyrighted assets and heavy external media files.
  • Session Privacy: High scores and audio preferences persist locally in your browser memory via localStorage. No telemetry trackers or user profiling.

The Games

We launch games only when they meet rigorous gameplay and performance standards. Our flagship title, Citrus Blitz, features authentic slice physics, dynamic blade swipe trails, combo multipliers, and procedural audio synthesis.

Feedback & Contributions

We are actively developing new original arcade games, including memory challenges and puzzle reflex games. If you have ideas, feedback, or discover an issue on your device, please reach out via our Contact page.
