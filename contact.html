Frequently Asked Questions

Are the games completely free to play?

Yes. All games hosted on Mini Game Arcade are 100% free with no in-app purchases, paid subscriptions, or locked content.

Do games work on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes. Every title is tested specifically for touch interfaces, gesture controls, and responsive screen scaling on Android and iOS devices.

How do I save my high score?

Your high scores are automatically saved in your browser's local memory (localStorage). No account creation is required.

How do I report a game glitch?

Please select "Bug Report" in the form above and mention your device, browser version, and the game you were playing.
