Frequently Asked Questions

Are all games free to play?

Yes. All titles on Mini Game Arcade are 100% free with no in-app purchases, paid passes, or locked levels.

Do games work on mobile and tablet devices?

Yes. Every title is built specifically to support multi-touch gestures, swipe velocity detection, and responsive canvas scaling on Android and iOS devices.

How are high scores saved?

High scores are stored directly in your browser's local memory via localStorage. They remain saved on your device across visits without requiring an account.

How do I report a glitch or performance issue?

Select "Bug Report" in the form above and mention your device model, browser version, and the game you were playing.
