Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. How we protect and respect your personal privacy.

1. Introduction

At Mini Game Arcade, we believe privacy is fundamental. This policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used, and how your privacy is preserved while browsing our site and playing our games.

2. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not require user accounts, email registration, passwords, or personal profiles to play any game on Mini Game Arcade. We do not collect or request:

  • Names, residential addresses, or phone numbers
  • Credit card or payment billing details
  • Social media credentials or contact lists
  • Precise geographical location coordinates

3. Browser Local Storage (localStorage)

Our games use your browser's client-side localStorage purely to remember your progress. This data remains on your physical device and is never sent to any database:

  • High Scores: Remembers your highest scores across game modes (e.g. citrusBlitz_high_arcade, citrusBlitz_high_classic) so your records persist across sessions.
  • Audio Settings: Remembers whether you chose to mute sound (e.g. citrusBlitz_muted).

You can reset this data at any time by clearing your browser cache or site storage in your browser settings.

4. Server Diagnostics & Log Files

Like standard web hosts (such as Vercel), server infrastructure may automatically record generic diagnostic metadata such as browser version, operating system, referring URL, and request timestamps. These logs are used solely to monitor site availability, prevent malicious DDoS attacks, and diagnose network errors.

5. Third-Party Advertising Standards

Mini Game Arcade may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense) to support free operation and hosting costs. Third-party ad vendors, including Google, use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on previous visits to this website or other sites on the web.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other destinations online.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising through aboutads.info.

6. Children's Privacy

Mini Game Arcade provides family-friendly, general-audience arcade mini-games. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
