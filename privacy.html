Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. How we protect and respect your privacy.

1. Introduction

At Mini Game Arcade (accessible at our public website), we are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors and players. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used, and how your privacy is safeguarded when you play our games and browse our website.

2. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not require user accounts, email registration, passwords, or personal profiles to play any game on Mini Game Arcade. We do not collect:

  • Names, home addresses, or phone numbers
  • Credit card or payment billing details
  • Social media account credentials
  • Precise GPS location data

3. Browser Local Storage (localStorage)

Our games utilize your browser's native localStorage feature for client-side functionality. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external server:

  • Game High Scores: To keep track of your best arcade scores (e.g. citrusBlitz_high_arcade, citrusBlitz_high_classic) so your achievements remain intact when you reload the page.
  • Sound Preferences: To remember whether you preferred audio muted or unmuted (e.g. citrusBlitz_muted).

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cache or site data in your browser settings.

4. Web Analytics and Log Files

Like most standard web hosting platforms (including Vercel), server logs may automatically record generic, non-personally identifiable diagnostic information such as your browser type, device operating system, referring URL, and the time of requests. These logs are used strictly for site reliability, performance optimization, and diagnosing network errors.

5. Third-Party Advertising Technologies

Mini Game Arcade may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense) to support the hosting and free operation of the website. Third-party ad vendors, including Google, use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

6. External Links

Our website may contain links to external resources or developer documentation. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party websites.

7. Children's Privacy

Mini Game Arcade offers family-friendly, general-audience arcade mini-games. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal changes. Updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contacting Us

If you have questions, inquiries, or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us through our Contact page.
