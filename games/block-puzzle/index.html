How to Play & Controls

🧩 Polyomino Dock

You are dealt 3 shapes at a time. Click or tap any dock slot (or press keys 1, 2, 3) to select a piece, then place it on the board.

💥 Line Clears & Combos

Fill any complete horizontal row or vertical column to clear it. Clearing multiple lines at once or chaining back-to-back clears earns huge combo multipliers!

🎯 Game Modes

Classic is an endless high-score battle until the board fills. Challenge tests you to clear 10 lines in fewest moves. Timed Blitz gives you 90 seconds!

⌨️ Keyboard Controls

Keys 1, 2, 3 select shapes. Arrow Keys / WASD move the grid cursor. Space / Enter places the shape. P to Pause, R to Restart.

📱 Touch & Mouse

Click or tap any dock shape to select it, then tap your target cell on the board. A translucent ghost preview shows legal placements and lines about to blast.

⭐ Strategy Tip

Avoid isolating empty single holes. Always keep space in the center open for large 3×3 squares or 1×5 long lines!

Frequently Asked Questions

When does a Block Puzzle game end?

The game ends when none of the 3 current polyomino pieces waiting in your dock can legally fit onto the remaining grid spaces.

How do row and column clears work?

Placing blocks that fill any complete 8-block horizontal row or vertical column instantly detonates the line and frees grid cells.

Can blocks be rotated?

No. Authentic block puzzle logic challenges your spatial planning by requiring you to place pieces in their given orientations.

What is the best strategy for a high score?

Keep the center of the grid open for large 3x3 pieces, and always aim to clear multiple rows or columns simultaneously for combo multipliers.
