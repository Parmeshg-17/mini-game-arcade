How to Play Water Sort

Water Sort is an elegant color-sorting logic puzzle designed to test your spatial reasoning, working memory, and forward planning. The objective is deceptively straightforward: group all liquids of the same color into their own individual glass test tubes until the entire laboratory is neatly organized.

Matching Colors Only
You can only pour liquid into a tube if its top color matches the color being poured, or if the destination tube is completely empty.
Tube Capacity Limit
Each glass tube holds a maximum of 4 liquid units. If a destination tube only has 1 empty slot remaining, only 1 unit will transfer.
Contiguous Transfer
When pouring, all contiguous matching units at the top of the source tube will transfer together, as long as there is enough space.
Empty Tube Buffer
Empty tubes act as crucial temporary holding bays. Guard your empty tubes carefully to avoid boxing yourself into an unsolvable corner.

Strategic Tips for Puzzle Masters

  • Clear a Tube Early: Having an empty tube gives you tremendous mobility. Always look for quick opportunities to empty a mixed tube completely.
  • Consolidate Large Blocks: Whenever you can unite two 2-unit segments of the same color, you instantly create a 4-unit completed tube, freeing up permanent board space.
  • Avoid Meaningless Shuffling: Pouring liquid into an empty tube without a concrete subsequent plan reduces your maneuvering room. Keep empty tubes clear until you need them.
  • Use Assists Responsibly: If you find yourself in a tight spot on expert levels, the Hint button calculates the provably best move, and +1 Tube adds an emergency glass vessel.
