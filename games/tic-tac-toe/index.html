Controls & Input

  • Touch / Mouse: Tap or click any open square on the 3x3 grid to claim it.
  • Keyboard Numbers: Press 1 through 9 to place a mark instantly.
  • Arrow Keys: Navigate cells with ↑ ↓ ← →, press Enter or Space to place.
  • Quick Reset: Press R anytime to wipe the board and start fresh.
  • Audio Toggle: Press M to mute or unmute synthesized arcade audio.
[ 🎮 ]

Arcade Game Modes

  • Me vs Bot: Play against our intelligent AI decision engine. Choose between approachable Casual Bot or the unbeatable Mastermind AI powered by recursive minimax depth pruning.
  • 2 Players (Pass & Play): Play head-to-head on the same device with a friend, alternating turns between Electric Lime (X) and Vibrant Coral (O).
  • Pick Your Symbol: Choose whether you want to move first with X or respond second with O.
[ ★ ]

Winning & Rules

  • Three in a Row: Connect three of your marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to trigger the neon victory laser strike.
  • Cat's Game (Draw): If all 9 squares are claimed without three matching marks, the match concludes in a draw.
  • Zero Server Latency: 100% computed in-browser using Web Audio synthesis and CSS hardware-accelerated animations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tic Tac Toe free to play online?

Yes, Tic Tac Toe is 100% free with zero downloads, accounts, or in-game purchases on desktop and mobile.

Can you defeat the Mastermind AI Bot?

The Mastermind Bot uses an unbeatable minimax algorithm with alpha-beta pruning. With optimal play the game ends in a tie, but Easy and Medium difficulties feature human-like mistakes you can exploit.

Can two players play on the same device?

Yes. Switch the game mode to 2 Players for local pass-and-play matches with friends on any phone, tablet, or laptop.

Are win/loss statistics saved?

Yes. Wins, losses, and draws are tracked locally in your browser memory via localStorage so your session records persist.
