Controls & Input

  • Touch / Mouse: Click or tap any face-down card to flip it over.
  • Keyboard Arrows: Navigate across cards with ↑ ↓ ← →.
  • Flip Card: Press Enter or Space to flip the focused card.
  • Quick Restart: Press R to shuffle a new deck.
  • Audio Toggle: Press M to mute or unmute procedural arcade audio.
[ 🎯 ]

3 Difficulty Modes

  • Easy (8 Cards): 4 matching pairs in a 4×2 grid. Fast, breezy, and perfect for quick warmups.
  • Medium (16 Cards): 8 matching pairs in a classic 4×4 grid. The sweet spot of memory strategy.
  • Hard (24 Cards): 12 matching pairs in an extensive 6×4 grid. The ultimate memory test.
[ ★ ]

Star Ratings & Scoring

  • 3 Stars (★★★): Finish near optimal par moves with minimal redundant flips.
  • Session Records: Your lowest move count and fastest completion time for each mode are saved in localStorage.
  • Zero Lag: 100% client-side with 3D CSS hardware transforms and zero server latency.

Frequently Asked Questions

What difficulty modes are available in Memory Match?

You can choose between Starter (4x3 grid, 12 cards), Medium (4x4 grid, 16 cards), and Mastermind (6x4 grid, 24 cards).

How is performance evaluated?

Scores are calculated based on your total elapsed completion time and the fewest card flips needed to pair all matching arcade emblems.

Can I play Memory Match on a smartphone?

Yes, cards adapt responsively to touchscreens with large tactile tap targets and smooth 3D flip card animations.

Are best records saved between visits?

Yes, your lowest move count and fastest completion times for each difficulty are saved locally on your physical device.
