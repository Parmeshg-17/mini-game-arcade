Controls & Input

  • Touch / Mouse: Click or tap any face-down card to flip it over.
  • Keyboard Arrows: Navigate across cards with ↑ ↓ ← →.
  • Flip Card: Press Enter or Space to flip the focused card.
  • Quick Restart: Press R to shuffle a new deck.
  • Audio Toggle: Press M to mute or unmute procedural arcade audio.
[ 🎯 ]

3 Difficulty Modes

  • Easy (8 Cards): 4 matching pairs in a 4×2 grid. Fast, breezy, and perfect for quick warmups.
  • Medium (16 Cards): 8 matching pairs in a classic 4×4 grid. The sweet spot of memory strategy.
  • Hard (24 Cards): 12 matching pairs in an extensive 6×4 grid. The ultimate memory test.

Star Ratings & Scoring

  • 3 Stars (★★★): Finish near optimal par moves with minimal redundant flips.
