🎮 Controls & Shortcuts

  • Mouse Drag / Touch Aim the trajectory laser line across the board.
  • Click / Release Fire the loaded bubble into the hex cluster.
  • Tap Next Bubble / S Swap the loaded bubble with the next in queue.
  • Arrow Keys Fine-tune shooting angle left and right.
  • P Pause or resume game.
  • R Restart current wave.
  • M Toggle sound effects.

⚡ Trick Shots & Mechanics

  • Wall Ricochets: Bounce shots off the left and right walls to slip behind obstacles!
  • Match-3 Rule: Connect 3 or more bubbles of the same color to burst them.
  • Avalanche Drops: Pop support clusters to drop unanchored islands for massive bonus points!
  • Power Boosters: Activate Bomb or Wild bubbles to blast through tough formations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you pop bubbles in Bubble Pop?

Aim your launcher and shoot bubbles to match 3 or more of the same color, popping them from the ceiling cluster.

What is an avalanche drop?

Any bubbles that become disconnected from the ceiling anchor points automatically collapse and fall for massive avalanche bonus scores.

Can you bounce bubbles off the walls?

Yes, bank shots off the left and right walls allow you to thread bubbles into hard-to-reach pockets and high clusters.

How do touch controls work on mobile?

Drag your finger on screen to guide the aiming trajectory laser, then release to launch the bubble with precision.
