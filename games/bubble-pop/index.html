🎮 Controls & Shortcuts

  • Click / Tap Tap any cluster of 2+ matching bubbles to pop.
  • Arrow Keys Navigate keyboard cursor across bubble cells.
  • Space / Enter Pop the highlighted cluster.
  • P Pause or resume game.
  • R Restart current match.
  • M Toggle sound effects on/off.

⚡ Scoring Rules & Combos

  • Cluster Math: Score scales quadratically: N × N × 10 points. 5 bubbles = 250 pts, 10 bubbles = 1,000 pts!
  • Combo Streaks: Pop clusters in rapid succession within 2.8s to ignite up to a 5× Combo Multiplier!
  • Board Clear Bonus: Eradicate every single bubble on the board for a colossal +2,500 Perfect Clear bonus!
  • Gravity Physics: Popped slots cause bubbles above to slide down, and empty columns slide left.
