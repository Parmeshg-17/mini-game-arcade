How to Play & Controls

🔗 Connect Matching Dots

Click or drag between two nodes of the same color to connect them with a continuous glowing wire. No two wires may cross or overlap at any point.

💯 100% Board Fill

The level is complete ONLY when every single cell on the board is covered! Leaving even one empty square means the circuit puzzle is not solved yet.

⭐ Efficiency Stars

Earn up to 3 Stars by connecting all paths without wasted moves (Par moves). Level progression and star achievements are saved locally.

💡 Smart Hint System

Progress stalled? Tap the Hint (H) button to automatically reveal and draw a verified solution path for one uncompleted color node!

⌨️ Desktop Controls

Arrow Keys / WASD move the grid cursor. Spacebar / Enter starts or steps drawing. 1-8 jumps to node. U to Undo, H for Hint, R to Restart.

📱 Mobile Controls

Touch and drag your finger across the grid from any neon dot. Dragging backward automatically backtracks cleanly. Calibrated for phones & tablets!
