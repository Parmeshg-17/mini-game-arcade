HOW TO PLAY

🎯
Controls:

Click & drag with your mouse or swipe across the screen with your finger to slash.

🍉
Juicy Fruits:

Watermelon, Orange, Lemon, Strawberry, Kiwi, and Pineapple award 1 to 3 points each.

⭐
Golden Starfruit:

Rare glowing fruit that grants +10 points and adds +5 bonus seconds to your clock!

💣
Rule: AVOID BOMBS!

Slicing a bomb detonates an explosion, deducts 10 seconds and 10 points (or 1 life in Classic mode), and resets your combo streak!

⚡
Combo Multipliers:

Slice 2 or more fruits in a single fluid swipe stroke to earn 3x, 4x, and 5x frenzy bonuses.

About Citrus Blitz

A fast-paced, original arcade fruit-slicing experience running directly in your browser with zero installation.

🎮 Controls & Keybindings

  • Mouse Drag Click & swipe cursor across fruits
  • Touch Swipe Swipe finger(s) across touchscreens
  • Space or P Pause / Resume game
  • R Restart current session
  • M Toggle sound on / off
  • Esc Close modal / Pause

✨ Game Modes & Rules

⚡ Arcade Blitz (60s)

Race against a 60-second timer. Slice fruits to rack up points. Slice rare Golden Starfruit for +10 points and +5 bonus seconds! Beware of bombs: slicing a bomb costs 10 seconds and 10 points.

❤️ Classic Survival (3 Strikes)

No timer! You have 3 lives. Missing any fruit costs 1 life. Slicing a bomb costs 1 life. Lose all 3 lives and the game ends.

🔥 Combo Streaks

Slice 2, 3, 4, or 5+ fruits in a single fluid stroke to unleash mega bonus scores and golden fire trails.
