About Citrus Blitz: Free Fruit Slicing Browser Game

Slice juicy watermelons, oranges, lemons, strawberries, and pineapples in this high-energy arcade action game. Master fluid gestures, trigger combo frenzy multipliers, and dodge explosive bombs directly in your browser.

Controls & Keybindings

  • Mouse Drag Click & swipe cursor across fruits
  • Touch Swipe Swipe finger across touchscreen displays
  • Space or P Pause / Resume game session
  • R Restart current session
  • M Toggle sound and arcade audio
  • Esc Close active modal or pause

Game Modes & Scoring Strategy

Arcade Blitz (60s Timer)

Race against a 60-second timer. Slice fruits to rack up points. Slice rare Golden Starfruit for +10 points and +5 bonus seconds. Beware: detonating a bomb deducts 10 seconds and 10 points.

Classic Survival (3 Strikes)

High-stakes precision. You start with 3 lives. Missing any fruit costs 1 life. Hitting a bomb detonates and costs 1 life. Lose all 3 lives and the session ends.

Multi-Slice Combo Frenzy

Slice 2, 3, 4, or 5+ fruits in a single unbroken stroke to trigger 3x, 4x, and 5x score multipliers with fiery visual trail effects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Citrus Blitz completely free to play?

Yes. Citrus Blitz is 100% free with no paywalls, accounts, or in-app purchases. You can launch and play directly in any web browser.

How do you trigger combo multipliers?

Slice two or more fruits in a single fluid gesture before lifting your mouse button or finger. Combos grant instant 3x, 4x, or 5x score multipliers.

What are the rules of Arcade Blitz vs Classic Survival?

Arcade Blitz is a 60-second time trial where bombs reduce your timer by 10s. Classic Survival gives you 3 lives where missing any fruit or striking a bomb costs 1 strike.

Can I play Citrus Blitz on mobile or tablet devices?

Yes. Built on responsive HTML5 Canvas, Citrus Blitz automatically adapts to iOS, iPadOS, Android smartphones, tablets, and touch-enabled PCs.

What does the Golden Starfruit do?

The rare glowing Golden Starfruit awards +10 bonus points and extends your Arcade Blitz timer by +5 seconds whenever sliced.

How does the collision physics work?

The game engine computes mathematical circle-to-line-segment intersections along your blade trajectory, providing crisp, sub-pixel slicing precision.
