🎮 Controls & Shortcuts

  • Click / Tap Click on any tile adjacent to the empty space to slide it.
  • Touch Swipe Swipe across the board in the direction you want tiles to slide.
  • Arrow Keys / WASD Slide tiles in four directions (← → ↑ ↓).
  • P Pause or resume puzzle timer.
  • R Reshuffle the board with a fresh 100% solvable layout.
  • M Toggle sound effects.

🧩 How to Play & Rules

  • Movement: Click on a tile adjacent to the empty space to slide it. Only tiles next to the empty space can be moved.
  • Objective: Arrange all numbers in order from 1 to 15.
  • Goal Position: The empty space should end up in the bottom-right corner.
  • Efficiency: Plan your moves carefully to solve the puzzle efficiently and challenge yourself to complete it in fewer moves!
  • Green Dot: When a tile shows a neon green dot, it is locked into its correct final target spot!
