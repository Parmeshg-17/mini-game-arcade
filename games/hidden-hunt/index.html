How to Play

Master the art of observation.

1. Inspect the Scene: Look at the target item icons and names listed on the bottom shelf.

2. Tap to Claim: When you spot a target item in the illustration, tap directly on it to collect it.

3. Zoom & Pan: Use the + / − buttons or touch drag to inspect tiny corners.

4. Need Help? Tap the 💡 Hint button to highlight one remaining item's exact spot.

Hidden Hunt is an observation puzzle game where players inspect detailed illustrated environments to locate specific target objects shown on the bottom shelf. Click or tap directly on the object once discovered to claim it.
Hidden Hunt features 3 initial worlds: The Old Library, Mystery Forest, and Pirate's Treasure. Each world contains 5 distinct hand-crafted scenes, offering 15 complete playable levels plus daily challenges.
