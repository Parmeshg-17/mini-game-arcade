Gameplay: Break, Fall, and Repeat

Each round in Stack Ball challenges you to guide a bouncing ball downward by destroying stacked circular platforms beneath it. Only colored layers allow safe landings and successful breaks, while touching any black segment instantly shatters the ball and forces a restart.

Smart timing creates consecutive piercings, which activate a powerful Fireball State that smashes through every stack without restriction! Levels grow more demanding through denser layouts and trickier color patterns, so reaching the checkered base safely becomes the ultimate goal.

Simple Controls
A single tap or click drops the ball one level. Holding the input plunges through multiple layers when conditions remain safe.
Fireball Streaks
Smashing multiple consecutive safe platforms builds momentum, transforming the ball into a flaming meteor that annihilates black hazard segments.
Dynamic Rotations
Each floor rotates at independent speeds and alternating directions, creating dynamic windows of opportunity to pierce downward.

Why Players Keep Bouncing

  • Instant Satisfaction: One-touch controls deliver immediate tactile feedback with every shattered layer.
  • Smooth Physical Destruction: Broken platform wedges tumble away with realistic outward velocity, spin, and gravity.
  • Strategic Risk & Reward: Fireball streaks change risk decisions, encouraging brave plunge streaks through safe patterns.
  • Escalating Challenge: Levels ramp up smoothly with denser hazard distributions without ever feeling unfair.

Win Faster, Smash Smarter

  • Wait Briefly Before Plunging: Anticipate the rotation to ensure a clean multi-layer opening before committing to a hold.
  • Chase Fireball Streaks: When you spot a cluster of safe colored arcs, plunge aggressively to ignite the fireball.
  • Release Before Black Segments: Releasing input immediately returns the ball to a safe bounce atop the platform.
  • Use Single Taps in Crowded Zones: When hazard black segments dominate, tap once to advance floor-by-floor carefully.
