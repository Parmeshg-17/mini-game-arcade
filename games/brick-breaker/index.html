Paddle Controls

  • Mouse / Touch: Move mouse or drag touch horizontally across the playfield to slide the paddle.
  • Keyboard Arrows: Use ← and → or A and D for smooth keyboard steering.
  • Launch / Pause: Press Space or P to launch the orb or pause the action.
  • Quick Reset: Press R to wipe the wave and restart.
  • Mute Audio: Press M to toggle synthesized audio effects.
[ ⚡ ]

Power-Up Capsules

  • Multi-Ball (●●): Spawns 2 additional energy orbs simultaneously to overwhelm the grid.
  • Wide Paddle (↔): Expands paddle surface by 45% for 12 seconds of forgiving defense.
  • Extra Shield (♥): Replenishes 1 lost shield heart up to a maximum of 5.
  • Slow Orbit (⏱): Calms ball speed for precision bank shots into tight gaps.
[ 🎯 ]

Reflection Physics & Combos

  • Angle Deflection: Hits closer to the paddle edge create sharp, aggressive rebound angles; center hits bounce straight up.
  • Combo Multipliers: Chain consecutive brick hits without paddle contact to multiply score up to ×5.
  • Armored Bricks: Purple durable bricks absorb one hit before cracking and yielding high bonus score.

Frequently Asked Questions

What power-ups appear in Brick Breaker?

Broken bricks can drop Multi-ball (spawns 2 extra orbs), Wide Paddle (expands paddle by 45%), Shield Heart (restores a lost life), and Slow Orbit (slows ball speed).

How does the paddle angle reflection work?

Hits near the paddle edges create sharp, fast rebound angles for horizontal bank shots, while center hits bounce vertically.

How do combo streaks work?

Hitting multiple bricks in succession before the ball touches the paddle multiplies your score up to 5x with neon visual flair.

What are armored bricks?

Purple armored bricks absorb an initial impact to crack before shattering on the second hit for high bonus points.
