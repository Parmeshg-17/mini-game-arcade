🎮 Flight Controls & Shortcuts

  • Touch / Mouse Drag Steer your cyber starship smoothly in any direction.
  • Arrow Keys / WASD Pilot the ship with momentum & banking tilt.
  • Space Deploy EMP Shockwave to vaporize all hazards on screen!
  • P Pause or resume flight.
  • R Restart current sector.
  • M Toggle sound effects.

⚡ Pilot Tactics & Hazard Guide

Frequently Asked Questions

What are near-miss hazard bonuses?

Piloting your ship within close pixel proximity of an incoming asteroid without colliding awards instant Near-Miss multiplier points.

Does the game speed increase over time?

Yes, survival time gradually increases asteroid spawn frequency, variety, and downward velocity to test your high-speed reflexes.

What controls are supported?

Steer using Left/Right arrows, A/D, mouse cursor tracking, or horizontal touch dragging on mobile devices.

How do I beat my high score?

Focus on staying near the lower-middle screen, making minimal precise adjustments, and intentionally grazing slower asteroids for near-miss points.
