How to Play Screw Master

Screw Master is an intricate mechanical logic puzzle that challenges your spatial reasoning, order-of-operations planning, and resource management. Overlapping wooden beams, brushed steel brackets, and brass plates are screwed securely into a workshop backing board. Your objective is to extract the screws in the exact strategic sequence needed to release every single panel.

Accessible Screws Only
You can only unscrew bolts that are exposed on top. If a higher overlapping panel covers a screw, you must remove that panel first.
Empty Hole Routing
A removed screw must have a valid destination hole. You can place screws into empty holes on the board or in the bottom storage tray.
Panel Release Physics
When all anchoring screws holding a panel are extracted, the panel automatically falls away, uncovering previously hidden holes.
Strategic Tray Storage
Your storage tray has limited capacity. Plan your moves carefully so you do not fill all slots before freeing up new board holes.

Strategic Tips for Mechanical Masters

  • Identify the Topmost Panel: Look for the panel on the highest visual layer. Since nothing covers it, its screws are always immediately accessible.
  • Free Up Board Holes Early: Dropping a panel clears the holes it was occupying, creating valuable new temporary parking spots for other screws.
  • Preserve Tray Slots: Never fill your storage tray unless you are certain the move will immediately trigger a panel drop.
  • Use Power-Ups Wisely: The Hint button calculates the mathematically optimal step using our internal solver, while +1 Slot gives you an extra tray holder.
