How to Play Jigpic Solitaire

🖱️
Click & Tap to Match
Click any card in your hand to select it, then click the matching grid position to snap it in. Or click an empty slot directly to auto-play if held in hand!
✋
Drag & Drop Support
Drag piece cards smoothly from the Solitaire tray directly into open slots on the picture canvas.
🎴
Draw Pile / Deck (D / Space)
Click the draw deck to cycle new cards into your hand when you need different segments of the picture.
👁️
Peek Original (P)
Toggle the Peek overlay at any point to view the completed master artwork as a reference guide.
⌨️
Keyboard Shortcuts
Keys 1, 2, 3 select hand cards. Arrow keys navigate grid slots. Enter places card. D draws, H hints, U undoes, R restarts.
🌟
Adjacency Bonus & Stars
Connecting a block adjacent to already placed neighbors awards bonus points. Solve in fewer moves for a 3-star rating!
