FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Merge Grid is a 4×4 sliding tile puzzle game. Use arrow keys, WASD, or swipe gestures to slide all tiles in one direction. When two tiles with the same number collide they merge into a tile worth double the value. The goal is to create a tile worth 2048.
Keep your highest-value tile anchored in a corner. Slide in a repeating L-shaped pattern to build a chain of doubling values: 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32 → 64 → 128 → 256 → 512 → 1024 → 2048. Avoid filling the board with small tiles that cannot be merged.
Yes. Merge Grid includes a one-move undo. Click or tap the Undo button in the HUD bar to revert your last slide and restore the previous board state and score. Use it wisely — only one undo is available at a time.
Every time two tiles merge your score increases by the value of the new tile. Merging two 64 tiles adds 128 to your score. Multiple merges in a single slide all count. Your personal best score is saved automatically in your browser's local storage.
Yes. Merge Grid is 100% free to play directly in your web browser with no downloads, accounts, or payments required. It runs on desktop and mobile devices and works across Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
When you create a 2048 tile a win overlay appears. You can choose to keep playing to reach even higher tiles like 4096 or 8192, or start a fresh game to chase a higher total score. The board and score are preserved when you continue.
