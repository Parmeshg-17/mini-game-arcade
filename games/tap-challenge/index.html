How to Play & Controls

⚡ Reflex Rush (30s)

Tap glowing nodes before their countdown ring expires. Hit golden stars for 300 pts, speed pulses for +1s time, and steer clear of glitch mines!

🔢 Number Sprint (1–16)

Scan the 4×4 grid and tap numbers 1 through 16 in exact numerical order. Accuracy is key—wrong taps add penalty time!

🔥 CPS Frenzy (10s)

A pure 10-second test of raw tapping frequency. Tap as fast as humanly possible to discover your Clicks Per Second tier!

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

Use Q, W, E / A, S, D / Z, X, C or Numpad 1–9 for the 3×3 grid pads. Press Space in Frenzy mode, P to Pause, R to Restart, and M to Mute.

📱 Touch & Mobile

Engineered with low-latency touch handlers and multi-touch support for tablets and smartphones. No zoom delay or tap lag.

⭐ 3-Star Ratings

Earn up to 3 stars by maintaining combo streaks in Reflex mode, finishing under 12s in Number Sprint, or hitting 8+ CPS in Frenzy.
