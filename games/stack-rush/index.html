How to Play & Controls

🔄 Rotate the Platform

The innovative twist! Slide your finger sideways or press A / D / Left / Right to spin the 3D bottom platform and align existing gaps with the falling piece.

💥 Destroy Complete Layers

Fill all 9 cells of any horizontal 3×3 layer to blast it into sparkling crystal fragments. Clear multiple layers at once to trigger massive combo multipliers!

💎 Gems & Visual Themes

Collect gems from block placements and layer blasts. Use your gems to unlock stunning visual themes like Obsidian Gold, Retro Synth, and Matrix Jade!

⌨️ Keyboard Controls

A / D or Left / Right to rotate platform. W / Up to rotate falling block. Space / Down to instantly drop. P to Pause, R to Restart.

📱 Touch Gestures

Swipe sideways anywhere on the 3D canvas to smoothly spin the platform. Tap the large Drop Block button below the stage to slam the block home.

🔮 Anticipate Ahead

Keep your eye on the NEXT block preview in the top-right corner. Rotate the tower to pre-build landing channels before the next piece arrives!
