How to Play & Controls

🎯 One-Button Timing

Blocks glide horizontally across alternating axes (X / Z). Tap anywhere on screen, click, or hit Spacebar to drop the block with pinpoint timing!

✂️ Precision Trimming

Poorly aligned edges are trimmed off and tumble away with realistic 3D physics. Line up within tolerance for PERFECT! alignments, harmonious chime streaks, and sparkle bursts!

📈 Increasing Difficulty

As your tower grows taller, oscillation speed accelerates and the target platform narrows. Chain 8 consecutive perfects to expand your block size back up!

⌨️ Desktop Controls

Spacebar / Enter / Down Arrow to drop block. P to Pause, R to Restart immediately on game over, M to Toggle Audio.

📱 Mobile Controls

Tap anywhere on the screen or 3D canvas to drop. Instant one-tap restart keeps gameplay fast and addictive on any phone or tablet.

🔮 Anticipate Ahead

Keep your eye on the NEXT block preview in the top-right corner. Rotate the tower to pre-build landing channels before the next piece arrives!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Stack Rush free to play online?

Yes, Stack Rush is 100% free to play directly in modern web browsers without installation, downloads, or sign-ups.

How do you rotate the 3D tower and drop blocks?

Use Left / Right arrow keys, A / D, or drag horizontally on screen to rotate the cylinder. Press Down / S for soft drop or Space for an instant hard drop.

How do layer clears and score combos work?

Filling an entire horizontal circular ring of blocks triggers a layer collapse. Clearing multiple layers simultaneously awards massive combo multipliers.

Can I play Stack Rush on mobile devices?

Yes. Stack Rush supports horizontal drag rotation, swipe drops, and on-screen tap controls optimized for mobile touchscreens.

What is the difference between soft drop and hard drop?

Soft drop accelerates block fall speed while letting you continue adjusting rotation. Hard drop locks the block instantly onto the surface below.

How does the NEXT piece preview help strategy?

The upcoming piece preview in the top HUD allows you to pre-rotate the tower to align landing zones before the block enters the cylinder.
